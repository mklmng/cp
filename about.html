A place to begin

Learning belongs to everyone.

Common Ground is a neighborhood studio for people who want to stay curious, build practical skills, and learn alongside others.

Our story

Less lecture.
More learning together.

We started Common Ground with a simple idea: useful learning should feel welcoming, social, and connected to real life. Our small-group workshops are led by people who love their craft and love helping others find their way into it.

There’s no perfect starting point here. Bring your questions, your half-formed ideas, and the willingness to try. We’ll take it from there.

What we believe

Make it practical

Learn something you can use, practice, and keep building on after class.

Make room for people

Different perspectives make a good class better. Everyone has something to bring.

Stay curious

Progress starts with a question. We value the experiments as much as the answers.
